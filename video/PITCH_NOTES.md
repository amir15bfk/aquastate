# Pitch notes — how to use the film

## The one sentence

**AquaState turns NASA Earth data into one water-climate index, then lets
you drill from the planet to your country to a single dam and answer:
are we covered until the next rain?**

## If you have 30 seconds

Play the film. Stop on the end card. Say only:

> "249 countries. Four live pressures. 82,394 dams. One index. And it runs
> on live NASA and open data, not a mock."

## If you have 2 minutes after the film

1. **World** — point at the global 61.5 and the four pressure cards. "This
   is not a static mock; the page refreshes and the snapshot is stamped."
2. **Algeria** — click the country. "45.6, Watch, rank 232. Drought 56,
   desertification 67. Here is the arithmetic behind the number."
3. **Beni Haroun** — open the dam. "81.0, Recharging. Observed 38.6,
   projected 83.3, buffer 0.950. It answers the same question at dam scale."
4. **Hazards / Sources** — show the event ledger and the source freshness
   table. "Every number carries a source and a timestamp."

## Three numbers to repeat

- **249** countries scored
- **82,394** catalogued dams (33 monitored for live status)
- **61.5** global WCRI (70 resilient / 108 stable / 62 watch / 9 stressed)

## What not to claim

- It is a **screening decision aid**, not an operational warning system.
- Reservoir storage is **modelled/blended**, not SCADA telemetry.
- Groundwater is a labelled **proxy**; GRACE is not ingested.
- Flood is **GloFAS river discharge**, not a local flood warning.
- The dam atlas is the **Wikidata corpus**, not a complete physical census.
- Do not claim NASA endorsement.

Judges reward the honesty more than a fake "AI predicts the future" line.
The app's whole design argument is that every number is traceable.

## 15-second elevator version

> "Water shaped every civilization. Climate change is now rewriting the map.
> AquaState uses NASA and open Earth data to score 249 countries and 82,000
> dams on one index — and shows the arithmetic. One screen answers: are we
> covered until the next rain?"

## Submission blurb

> AquaState is a world water and climate observatory built on NASA POWER /
> MERRA-2, NASA EONET, GloFAS and the World Bank. Its WCRI-2.0 index scores
> 249 countries across water availability, drought, groundwater, flood,
> wildfire and desertification; the dam layer carries 82,394 catalogued
> structures and 33 live-monitored pilot dams. Every score exposes its
> formula, inputs, source, confidence and freshness. The 30-second film is a
> cold open for the demo: river origins, drought, flood, then the product and
> the one question it answers.
