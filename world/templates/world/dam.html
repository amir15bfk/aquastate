Method note

What this page does and does not claim.

The DRI is the space app's signed HRI-1.0 mathematics, ported unchanged: SSI, CMI, SRRI, SAI and EESI, blended through the buffer bridge Φ. SAI is not scored for pilot basins because GRACE's footprint is coarser than these basins. Reservoir storage is a screening model/reference estimate, not telemetry. Scenario values are simulated and never overwrite the observed snapshot.
