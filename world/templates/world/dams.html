Coverage is honest: the atlas is built from the full Wikidata dam corpus with coordinates — 91,568 coordinate statements collapsing to {{ atlas_total|intcomma }} unique dam items — supplemented by curated public inventories. English labels are available for most rows; a minority fall back to their Wikidata ID. Capacity or power is available for a smaller notable subset. It is a catalogue, not an operational inventory or a guarantee that every small structure on Earth is present. Monitored dams have a modelled/live status.
