The cascade

Drought does not stay a drought.

Dry soil raises fire risk. Fire and heat strip the land. Hard ground sheds the next storm as a flood. AquaState shows the four pressures together and applies a small, explicit cascade penalty when drought, fire and desertification are all severe. The formula is on every country page.
